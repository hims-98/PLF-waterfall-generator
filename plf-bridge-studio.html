Type is optional: start, change, subtotal or total. Without a type, the first row becomes the start, a last row named Actual or Total becomes the end, and the rest are changes. Values like (0.85) count as negative.

#LabelValueType

Start and Absolute value bars are typed values drawn from the axis. Change bars float. Subtotal and Total are calculated from the running sum.
